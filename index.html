Four steps between a listing and a handshake.

1

Sellers verify first

Government ID, a video call with our staff, and a few questions. Review takes a minimum of two days.

2

Items list at a price

No bidding, no haggling in public. The price you see is the price on the record.

3

Meet halfway

Tell us your general area. We suggest a vetted spot roughly midway between you and the seller.

4

Confirm and release

When you are both at the spot, the app asks each of you to confirm the other. Then money moves.
