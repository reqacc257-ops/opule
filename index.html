The Opulé approach

01 · Know the seller

Sellers apply for identity and video-call review before listing. Review takes at least two days.

02 · A clear price

Discuss condition and accessories in chat. Agree on the price and meeting intent together.

03 · Meet thoughtfully

Choose and confirm a public venue together. Check its branch, opening hours, and seating.

04 · Inspect in person

Inspect before accepting. The sample handover is a simulation, not real escrow or payment.
